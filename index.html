Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

What is Spades Universe?

A complete Spades platform for learning, CPU practice, multiplayer competition, progression, rewards, and community.

Can I practice against the computer?

Yes. War Table lets you practice whenever you want against personality-driven CPU opponents.

Does the app teach Spades strategy?

Yes. Spades University is designed to explain bidding, card play, teamwork, table awareness, and game strategy.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Private rooms and multiplayer experiences are designed for playing with friends and other players.
